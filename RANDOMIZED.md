# What is randomized

With the default settings, a seed shuffles the chests, the gifts of every group but **Malon's Egg**, the loose items, the songs among themselves, and the stones and medallions among the bosses and Rauru. **Shop goods** and **Gold Skulltulas** start at **Vanilla**. Change any of it in **Settings…**.

## Chests

A seed moves the items that chests hold, in the overworld and in every dungeon, including the chests only the remake has. Every seed can be finished.

Kaepora checks that each item you need can be reached with what you find before it.

In **Settings…**, **Shuffled dungeons** lists the twelve dungeons. A dungeon you untick keeps the items the game has in its chests. The overworld has no such choice.

Some chests stay as the game has them. [The chest table](https://github.com/Kaepora-HQ/kaepora/blob/main/remake/chests.tsv) lists every chest in the game, and gives the reason for each one that is not shuffled.

## Keys, maps and compasses

A dungeon's small keys, boss key, map and compass stay inside that dungeon, in any of its chests, or at one of its loose spots when **Loose items** is **Shuffled**.

**Small and boss keys** has two other choices. **In their own chests** leaves each key where the game has it. **Not needed** starts the game with every small-key door unlocked and every boss key in hand.

**Maps and compasses** has the same first two choices. Its third, **Given at the start**, hands you every map and compass at once.

## Shops

**Shop goods** puts the shelves of the eight shops into the shuffle. **One per shop shuffled** to **Seven per shop shuffled** shuffles that many shelves in every shop with the other items, and **A random number per shop** picks from one to seven for each shop. A shop can then sell the Longshot, and a red rupee joins the shuffled items for each shuffled shelf. **Goods shuffled among the shops** shuffles no shelf with the other items and only deals shop goods out over the shelves again, so a shop can sell what another one sold. With **Vanilla**, shops sell what they always sold.

With any choice but **Vanilla**, the shelves that hold no shuffled item are filled from one list of shop goods, on shelves in any shop. The more shelves you shuffle, the shorter the list. A seed always sells the Deku Shield, the Hylian Shield, the Goron Tunic, the Zora Tunic, 5 Deku Nuts, 20 Bombs, 10 Bombchus and a Deku Stick somewhere. A few goods are not on the list and are sold nowhere: 10 Deku Seeds, Five Deku Sticks, the Magic Jar, Half-Full Lon Lon Milk, and the Bombchu, Three, Five, Eight, 15 and 30 Bombchus. Goods stay for sale after you buy them. Each costs its usual price on any shelf, so the lower price one shop asked for bombs, a fish or the Hylian Shield is gone. A shelf shows how many a pack holds only where that shelf had a sign before. The Goron Shop and the Zora Shop sell a tunic only to adult Link. The Kokiri Shop sells one to a child too, who cannot wear it.

**Shop prices** sets what the shuffled shelves cost. **Cheap** gives each its own price, from nothing up to 95 rupees, and **Balanced** goes up to 295. **Vanilla** keeps the price the shelf had. **One price** gives every shuffled shelf the price you type, and **Between two prices** picks each one between the two you type, up to 995 rupees. A seed never asks you to pay more than your wallet can hold when you get there. With **Goods shuffled among the shops** no shelf is shuffled with the other items, so **Shop prices** is switched off.

## Gold Skulltulas

Set **Gold Skulltulas** to **In dungeons**, **In the overworld** or **All**, and what those skulltulas leave behind joins the shuffle. A skulltula still leaves a gold token when it dies, and taking it gives you the seed's item.

The tokens they would have given join the shuffled items, so a token can turn up in a chest. A dungeon you untick under **Shuffled dungeons** keeps its skulltulas as the game has them. With **Vanilla**, every skulltula gives its token.

## Gifts

Under **Shuffle**, **Gifts** opens a list. Tick a group, or set **Deku Scrubs**, and what those characters and minigames hand over joins the shuffle. A group left unticked, or at **Vanilla**, gives what the game gives. By default every group but **Malon's Egg** is ticked, and **Deku Scrubs** is at **One Time Rewards**.

- **Minigames:** both fishing prizes, the Zora diving game, horseback archery's two prizes, Bombchu Bowling's two prizes, the frogs' two prizes, the ocarina memory game in the Lost Woods, both shooting galleries, the target in the Lost Woods, the rolling Goron in Goron City, Talon's cuccos, Anju's cuccos and Dampé's digging tour.
- **Characters:** the Skull Kid, the man on the roof, the lost dog's owner, the Big Poe buyer and the lakeside laboratory's dive.
- **House of Skulltula:** the five rewards for Gold Skulltula tokens.
- **Great Fairies:** the gifts of the six Great Fairies.
- **Story gifts:** the scene after the carpenters' rescue, Darunia's dance, his son's thanks once you stop him rolling, King Zora's thanks and Zelda's scene in the Temple of Time.
- **Malon's Egg:** what Malon hands you at the castle.
- **Biggoron Quest:** what Anju hands you as an adult, and what Biggoron hands you for his receipt.
- **Deku Scrubs**, set to **One Time Rewards:** the Deku theater's two prizes, the two Deku Scrubs that sell a bigger bag, and the two that sell a Piece of Heart.

Each bigger quiver, Deku-seed bag, bomb bag, Deku-stick bag and Deku-nut bag is then an item of its own and can turn up elsewhere. A bigger quiver or Deku-seed bag is not a weapon: you still need the Fairy Bow or the Fairy Slingshot to shoot.

You still do what the game asks for each one. What you are handed is the seed's item. The things they would have given join the shuffled items, so the Golden Scale can turn up in a chest. A gift may be the Fairy Bow or the Fairy Slingshot, and so may what a Gold Skulltula or a loose spot gives. Each comes with its quiver or bullet bag and 30 arrows or seeds, as it does from a chest, unless you already hold a bigger quiver or bullet bag: then the weapon comes alone.

The Gerudo Pass opens nothing by itself. Once you free the last carpenter, the Gerudo let you through Gerudo Fortress, into the Gerudo Proving Grounds and out to the Mirage Desert, whatever you were handed. The Rainbow Bridge appears once Zelda has met you in the Temple of Time, whatever she gave you, and you still need the Light Arrows to beat Ganondorf.

With **Malon's Egg** ticked, her egg is one of the shuffled items, and Malon at the castle hands you the seed's item. You need both the egg and her talk, in either order. Once you have both, the egg hatches into the Chicken by itself after about a minute and a half, or at once at the top of the vines on the way up to the castle.

With **Biggoron Quest** ticked, Anju as an adult and Biggoron on Death Mountain hand you the seed's items, and the Biggoron Receipt and the Biggoron Sword are among the shuffled items. The Pocket Egg is not, so the trade that starts with it never begins. Show Biggoron the receipt for his item. He is ready three game days after your game begins, and until then he tells you it is still in the works. He hands his item over once, and his lines still speak of the sword whatever he holds. Anju makes her offer whether you see her before him or after.

A Deku Scrub still asks its price: 40 rupees by the bridge in the Lost Woods and in the Lost Woods grotto, 100 in the Hyrule Field grotto and in Death Mountain Crater. The one in the Hyrule Field grotto sells only to young Link, and only once adult Link has read the password on the tree outside.

A Great Fairy still asks for her offering, and each but the summit's for Din's Fire as well, as she does in the game. What she hands you is the seed's item, and she hands it over once.

## Songs

With **Songs** at **Shuffled among themselves**, its default, the twelve songs change places. Every place that teaches a song still teaches one, so Impa may teach the Minuet of the Forest and Malon may teach Saria's Song.

Two things change with it. If you learn the Requiem of Spirit early and warp to the Evil Desert Colossus, the Spirit Temple and the way into the Mirage Desert stay shut there until you have walked into the desert from Gerudo Fortress. Without that, the early visit could cost you the Gerudo Pass. Until then nobody walks out of the Colossus, so warp there only once you know another warp song. The same goes for the Bolero of Fire and the middle of Death Mountain Crater, which an adult leaves only with the Hookshot, the Hover Boots or a grown bean plant.

A seed with shuffled songs also skips the short scene outside the Temple of Time after Link first draws the Master Sword. An adult who warped away before seeing it would otherwise have no map.

With **Vanilla**, every song is where the game has it.

## Loose items

With **Loose items** at **Shuffled**, its default, the items that lie in the open join the shuffle: 14 heart pieces, the eight heart containers the bosses leave, the Fire Arrow at Lake Hylia, the bottle at the bottom of Lake Hylia, and the loose small keys in the Bottom of the Well and the Shadow Temple. The two keys stay where they are while **Small and boss keys** is **In their own chests**. Ruto's Letter itself is always in a chest, and a hint calls it a Bottle, as the game does.

The seed's item lies where the game's did, and you take it by walking into it. A heart piece, a heart container and an item the seed left in its own spot are the game's own, and you take them as the game has you. A boss still shows a heart container as it falls, and what lies there afterwards is the seed's item. A dungeon you untick under **Shuffled dungeons** keeps its own. With **Vanilla**, each spot holds what the game has.

## Dungeon rewards

Nine places hand over a Spiritual Stone or a medallion: the eight dungeon bosses and Rauru. With **Dungeon rewards** at **Shuffled among themselves**, its default, those nine hand over the nine rewards in a new order, so Queen Gohma may leave you the Fire Medallion. Set it to **Shuffled**, and the nine places join the shuffle with everything else: a boss can then leave you the Hookshot, and a stone or a medallion can turn up somewhere else. A dungeon you untick under **Shuffled dungeons** keeps its boss's own. The scene still shows the dungeon's own stone or medallion.

The stones and medallions open nothing by themselves: the doors and scenes the story ties to them wait for their bosses, as the game has it. With **Vanilla**, each boss gives what the game has.

## Hints

With **Gossip stones** at **Give hints**, a stone speaks without the Mask of Truth, and most stones say something true about your seed.

A stone may name the place an item went, say that a place is on the way to finishing the game, or tell you an area holds nothing you need. A few only joke. The hints are in English. With the game in another language, the stones keep the game's own rumours.

With **Vanilla**, the stones are as the game has them, and ask for the mask.

With **Hints from characters** at **Give hints**, Mido tells you where the Kokiri Sword went when he stops you on the way to the Deku Tree. With **Kokiri Forest** at **Open** he never stops you, so he gives no hint. If you start with the Kokiri Sword, he has nothing to tell and keeps his own lines. With **Vanilla**, he keeps his own lines.

Whatever you choose, the owner of the Treasure Chest Game names the prize your seed put there, since his own line would be wrong.

## How the game starts

Four settings open the world sooner. They cover the way out of Kokiri Forest, the Door of Time, the Rainbow Bridge and Ganon's trials.

- **Kokiri Forest.** **Closed** is the game as you know it. **Closed Deku** opens the way out of the forest from the start, and Mido still wants a sword and a shield before he lets you through to the Deku Tree. **Open** opens both. With **Closed Deku** or **Open**, a seed may never send you into the Deku Tree: the Ocarina of Time waits for King Dodongo and Barinade, not for Queen Gohma, and so does a **Closed** Door of Time.
- **Door of Time.** **Closed** is the game as you know it: the door opens when you play the Song of Time at the altar, once Zelda has given you the Ocarina of Time. **Open** has the door standing open from the start, so you can pull the Master Sword and grow up at any time. Zelda, Impa, the Ocarina of Time and the Song of Time still wait for you as a child, and you can put the sword back to be a child again. If you beat King Dodongo as an adult first, Darunia thanks you with adult Link drawn at a child's size.
- **Rainbow Bridge.** **After the Light Arrows** is the game's own rule. **Always open** has the bridge standing from the start.
- **Ganon's trials.** **All six** leaves the barrier around Ganon's Tower to you. **None** takes it away.

## What a seed skips

Under **Start**, **Skip** lists what a seed leaves out of the game. Every box starts checked. Uncheck one to keep that part as the game has it.

- **Opening movie.** The narrated movie and the scene of Navi waking Link. A new game starts with Link in his house and Navi beside him, about 15 seconds after you begin. The whole opening takes about six minutes.
- **Player skippable scenes.** Every scene the game would let you skip. The seed skips each one the moment it starts, as if you had pressed Skip: the screen goes black for a moment and the game goes on. Boss introductions and part of the credits are among them. The scene of Navi waking Link is one too, so it is skipped even with **Opening movie** unchecked.
- **New area scenes.** The camera's first look over a place, which the game plays the first time you arrive and gives you no way to skip. The map still marks the place, though its name may not show. The owl's talk on your first walk to Hyrule Castle goes with it, and so do Rauru's words about the barriers on your first walk into Ganon's Castle.
- **Tutorial messages.** The lessons the game stops you for, such as the two at the Kokiri Shop that teach targeting and the shield, Navi's talk on your first walk into the Deku Tree, and the guide screens that follow your first find of an item. Two lessons still play: the Potion Shop's in the Market, which hands you a bottle, and the fishing pond's.
- **Saria's greeting.** Saria's welcome outside Link's house, with the forest's first scene before it. If you start with the Kokiri Sword, unchecking this box brings back only her talk: the game leaves out the forest's scene and her run-up for a Link who holds the sword.

Other scenes in which someone talks to you in a text box, and item, song and ocarina scenes, still play.

## What you start with

Under **Start**, **Items** opens a list. Tick an item and every new game of that seed begins with it. The seed then keeps that item out of the shuffled places and puts a small prize in the pool in its stead.

Ten items are boxes: the Kokiri Sword, the three shields, the Iron and Hover Boots, the Goron and Zora Tunics, the Megaton Hammer and the Boomerang. A seed still places its Deku Shields, as a Deku Shield can burn.

Nine more come in steps, and each has a row where you choose how far up it starts: the Fairy Slingshot, the Fairy Bow and the Bomb Bag by how much they hold; the Hookshot or the Longshot; the Goron Bracelet or the Silver or Golden Gauntlets; the Silver or Golden Scale; the Adult's or Giant's Wallet; Deku Sticks and Deku Nuts by how many you can carry. A step includes the ones before it, and a bag starts full. The last row is the Fairy Ocarina.

A place that is not shuffled and always gives one of these, such as Saria on the bridge, still gives it, though you hold it already. An item for adult Link shows grey in the menu until you are an adult.

## The Lens of Truth

**Lens of Truth** decides whether a seed may make you find the Lens before it asks you to cross what you cannot see.

With **Needed**, it may. With **Never needed**, no seed waits for the Lens, so you may have to find your way past what is hidden without it. The Treasure Chest Game's prize always takes the Lens, so with **Never needed** it stays as the game has it.

## What else a seed changes

A seed changes a few rules of the game, so that no order of play leaves it unfinishable.

- **Strength only goes up.** In the game, a weaker strength item picked up after a stronger one lowers what Link can lift. With a seed it never goes down.
- **Diving only gets deeper.** In the game, a Silver Scale picked up after the Golden Scale lowers how deep Link dives. With a seed it never goes down.
- **The Master Sword goes back at any time.** In the game, adult Link can return the sword and be a child again only once the Forest Temple is done. With a seed he can from the start, and Sheik waits behind the pedestal, out of the way. One thing to watch: a child who warps to the Evil Desert Colossus or into Death Mountain Crater cannot walk out. Learn a warp song that lands somewhere else first: the Minuet, the Serenade, the Nocturne or the Prelude.
- **Bombs need no strength.** Link can take a bomb out of the bag before he has a strength item.
- **The Kokiri Sword chest gives only what it holds.** In the game, the scene after that chest puts a Kokiri Sword in Link's hand, whatever the chest held. With another item in the chest, a seed leaves the scene out.
- **Darunia's son gives whenever you reach him.** In the game he hands over his gift only if you stop him rolling before you go on to the crater. With a seed he still gives after you have been to the crater or finished the Fire Temple, so what he hands over is never lost.
- **Time runs from the start.** In the game the afternoon stops at 3 PM until Impa sends you off from the castle, so before you meet Zelda the day never turns to night on its own. With a seed the day and night turn from the beginning.
- **A few doors and scenes.** Four dungeon doors start open. A seed lets you do things in an order the game never expected, so some scenes are changed to keep up. Story scenes, the Zora diving game and your first walk into Zora's Domain no longer set a quest back. King Zora stays frozen after the Water Temple until you thaw him, so what he hands over is never lost. If you finish both stone dungeons before you meet Zelda, her escape plays as soon as Impa has sent you off, and the drawbridge is no longer raised before it.
